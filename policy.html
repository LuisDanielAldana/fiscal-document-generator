Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fiscal Document Generator service ("we", "our", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our WhatsApp bot to request Mexican tax (SAT) documents.

Information We Collect

  • WhatsApp contact information: your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, used to identify you and deliver documents.
  • Fiscal identifiers: your RFC (Registro Federal de Contribuyentes), used to request documents on your behalf.
  • Fiscal e-signature credentials (FIEL): your .cer certificate, private key file, and the associated password, provided by your company administrator and stored encrypted. These are used solely to authenticate requests to the Mexican tax authority's document API and are never shared or displayed in plain text.
  • Generated documents: compliance opinions and fiscal status certificates generated on your behalf, stored securely for delivery via WhatsApp.
  • Conversation and message logs: message metadata (timestamps, message IDs, delivery status) used for auditing and preventing duplicate processing.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and determine which fiscal documents you're authorized to access.
  • To request and generate the fiscal documents you ask for via WhatsApp.
  • To deliver those documents back to you through WhatsApp.
  • To maintain audit logs for security and troubleshooting.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share data only with:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • SatGo (third-party fiscal document API) — to submit your FIEL credentials and RFC in order to retrieve documents directly from Mexico's tax authority (SAT).

We do not share your data with any other third party except as required by law.

Data Storage & Security

  • FIEL passwords are encrypted at rest.
  • Certificate and key files are stored in private, access-restricted storage — never publicly accessible.
  • Temporary local copies of credential files used during document generation are deleted immediately after each request.
  • Generated document links shared via WhatsApp expire after a short time window.

Data Retention

We retain your fiscal credentials and generated documents for as long as your account remains active with our service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To make such a request, or if you have any questions about this policy, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
